Cell Bypass Explorer

How much does switching individual cells in and out of a series string buy, against a fixed string, for REON EBS blocks (30 kW / 60 kWh) at a Massachusetts commercial site, and what is it worth to the owner? Chemistry-agnostic: the cell-to-cell spread is an input. Move any assumption; the simulation reruns in your browser.

Value for a Massachusetts C&I owner

Revenue is computed each year from the simulated usable energy at the AC meter: ConnectedSolutions pays on average kW held through events, demand charges on the monthly peak, Clean Peak on MWh discharged at peak. Values are medians over the simulated packs. Every input is in "Application: Massachusetts C&I site", with its source.

Where cell-level control pays: cell-to-cell spread

Extra lifetime energy (and service life) against the fixed string as the within-pack spread widens. Above the red line, the energy gain outweighs the extra BMS cost per delivered MWh. 1× is the spread fitted to the paper's cells. Wider spreads stand for looser binning, hot spots, mixed-age or second-life packs (illustrative, not measured).

Lifetime optimization: when to rest a cell

Resting takes a cell out of the string for a month so it ages on calendar time only. Cells are ranked by predicted remaining life (their own 12-month fade trend blended with a fade-proportional pack prior) and rested only before the pack's measured health falls to the stop level. The threshold is an output of the tool, not a guess.

Where the numbers come from. Cell-to-cell aging spread (rates, knee timing, post-knee slopes and their correlations) is calibrated on per-cell field data from Zhou et al., Nature Energy (2026); the model treats chemistry through the spread multiplier. String design (250 cells, about 10% backups), BMS cost (+4–6% of system cost), sensing accuracy and cell type are from REON's CTO. Rates: Eversource 2026 rate summary (effective Sep 1, 2026), Mass Save ConnectedSolutions materials, mass.gov Clean Peak. Costs: NREL ATB (fixed O&M 2.5%) and NREL's 2025 battery cost projections; ITC schedule under current law. CO₂: ISO-NE (2024) and Fraunhofer (2025). Assumed: the stationary timeline, switch loss, round-trip and inverter efficiency, installed cost, demand-charge capture (50%) and peak duration, cell mass, visit cost and cell failure rates (no public field data; shown as a range). The browser runs few packs for speed; at 1,000 packs it matches the Python model (+12.3% vs +12.1% energy, +$4.37k vs +$4.34k 20-year value). Estimates under stated assumptions, not REON performance claims. Model code: github.com/annabelleywu/mitec2026.
