Where the numbers come from. Cell-to-cell aging spread (rates, knee timing, post-knee slopes and their correlations) is calibrated on per-cell field data from Zhou et al., Nature Energy (2026), NMC car cells. The stationary timeline, the sodium-ion transfer, sensor noise, the OCV curve and every fault rate are placeholders. Estimates under stated assumptions, not REON performance claims.
